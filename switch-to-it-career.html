What is RPA? Explained Like You're 10

Robotic Process Automation (RPA) is not a metal robot walking in a factory. It is a smart software assistant inside your computer that mimics human keyboard clicks and mouse movements.

😫 Manual Work (Before RPA)

You spend 4 to 6 hours every day copying rows from Excel sheets, logging into websites, typing the same data, and downloading invoices. It is exhausting, repetitive, and full of human copy-paste errors.

🚀 With UiPath Bot (After RPA)

You hit Run (or schedule it). The UiPath software robot opens your apps, reads the files, completes all 500 entries in 30 seconds with zero errors, and sends you a completed summary.
