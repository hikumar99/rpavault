FAQ

Common questions

How fast can we get a shortlist?

For common automation and data roles, typically within 3–5 business days. Niche or senior roles can take a little longer — we’ll give you a realistic timeline upfront.

What if the hire doesn’t work out?

Placements include a support window with replacement cover — the exact terms are agreed per engagement before we start.

Do you only staff RPA roles?

RPA and automation are our core, but we also place data/BI, QA automation and general IT roles through the same delivery-first screening.

Can you staff an entire team?

Yes — that’s our Managed Pods model: an RPAVault-led lead-plus-developers unit that owns a defined scope end to end.
