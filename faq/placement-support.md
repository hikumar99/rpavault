## Placement Assistance & Career Support Program

We do not just teach you how to build bots or write SQL; we prepare you to get hired. Our **Career Services Program** is built into all our core certification tracks and starts in the final weeks of your training.

Here is the exact step-by-step placement support you receive.

### 1. Resume & Profile Optimization
* **ATS-Friendly Resumes:** We review your resume, re-structure it to align with industry keywords, and teach you how to translate your previous domain knowledge (even if non-IT) into automation/data accomplishments.
* **LinkedIn Makeover:** We optimize your LinkedIn profile headings, summaries, and projects to ensure recruiters find you during active talent searches.

### 2. Mock Technical Interviews
* **Architect Reviews:** You will undergo at least 2 mock interviews conducted by senior technical architects.
* **Detailed Feedback:** You receive a structured scorecard rating your communication, coding logic, tool understanding, and problem-solving, with precise areas for improvement.

### 3. Referral Pipeline & Job Alerts
* **Direct Referrals:** We partner with mid-sized IT consultancies, system integrators, and enterprise staffing agencies. When our partners have open junior/mid positions, we refer our top-performing certified students directly.
* **Job Support:** We guide you on how to apply for openings on platforms like Naukri, Indeed, and LinkedIn, helping you customize your cover letters for higher response rates.
