## Non-IT Background Eligibility Criteria

Yes, you are 100% eligible. In fact, over 80% of our successful students who have transitioned into high-paying corporate roles come from non-IT backgrounds—including Sales, Human Resources, Finance, Mechanical/Electrical Engineering, Customer Operations, and Administration.

Here is a detailed breakdown of why your non-IT background is not a barrier, and how we help you bridge the gap.

### 1. Low-Code vs. High-Code
Modern enterprise tools like **UiPath**, **Microsoft Power Automate**, and **Power BI** are designed as "low-code" platforms. Instead of writing thousands of lines of complex syntax from scratch (like in Java or C++), you build processes using visual flowcharts, drag-and-drop actions, and analytical logical blocks. If you can understand basic logic (e.g., *"If an invoice is marked as paid, move it to folder A; otherwise, email the customer"*), you can build professional automations.

### 2. The Value of Domain Knowledge
Companies do not just hire automation developers who can write code; they hire people who understand business processes. 
* If you have a **finance background**, you understand how accounts payable work, making you the perfect person to automate invoice processing.
* If you have a **logistics background**, you understand shipping manifests, making you ideal for automating supply chain operations.
Your domain experience is an asset, not a drawback.

### 3. Our Step-by-Step Training Methodology
We start our training programs from the absolute fundamentals:
* We teach you how to think like a developer before writing any code.
* We cover core database structures (SQL) and basic scripting parameters step-by-step.
* We provide 100% hands-on laboratory exercises with real-world corporate files.
* We guide you through building a portfolio of projects to prove your skills to recruiters.
