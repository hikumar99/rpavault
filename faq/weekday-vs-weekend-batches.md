## Weekday vs. Weekend Batch Schedules

We offer flexible learning schedules designed to fit the busy routines of both full-time students and working professionals. Here are the exact timings and details for our active training tracks.

### 1. Weekday Morning Batches
These sessions are highly recommended for fresh graduates, job seekers, and professionals with evening shifts.
* **Days:** Monday to Friday.
* **Timings:** 7:30 AM to 9:00 AM IST.
* **Duration:** 1.5 hours per day (1 hour live training + 30 minutes Q&A/Lab support).
* **Format:** Live interactive online sessions.

### 2. Weekend Batches
These sessions are custom-tailored for working professionals who cannot commit to learning during workdays.
* **Days:** Saturday and Sunday.
* **Timings:** 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM IST or 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM IST (depending on the course track).
* **Duration:** 3 hours per day (including breaks and interactive laboratory tasks).

---

### What happens if I miss a live class?
All live sessions are recorded in high-definition. 
1. **LMS Dashboard:** Recordings are uploaded to your student portal within 2 hours of the class finishing.
2. **Lifetime Access:** You have lifetime access to the recordings, slides, and code repositories.
3. **Flexible Attendance:** If you miss a weekday session, you can request a makeup token to attend the same module in the parallel weekend batch.
