Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026. This policy outlines how RPAVault collects, protects, and processes your personal data in accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and applicable international standards.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when expressing an interest in our services, requesting a callback, submitting course enrollment forms, or engaging with our career counselor assistant. This includes:

  • Contact Details: Full name, email address, mobile number, and WhatsApp contact.
  • Inquiry Context: Course preferences, career transition background, staffing requirements, or enterprise automation goals.
  • Technical Metadata: Anonymized IP addresses, browser types, device information, and approximate geographic location (city/country) to optimize performance and prevent automated spam.

2. Legal Grounds for Processing

Under the DPDP Act 2023 and GDPR, we process your personal data based on:

  • Your Express Consent: When you submit a callback, demo registration, or course inquiry form.
  • Contractual Necessity: To deliver educational training tracks, issue access to class materials, and coordinate consulting arrangements.
  • Legitimate Interests: To improve our curriculum, enhance website security, and maintain accurate records of learner communication.

3. Analytics, Meta Pixel & Cookies

We use Google Analytics (GA4) and the Meta Pixel to gather aggregate statistical information about site navigation and conversion trends. These tools help us gauge curriculum popularity and show relevant course information to prospective students. You can review our full cookie disclosures or learn how to manage your tracking preferences in our dedicated Cookie Policy.

4. Third-Party Data Processors

RPAVault does not sell, rent, or trade your personal details with third-party data brokers. We share information only with trusted cloud infrastructure providers necessary to fulfill your requests:

  • Cloudflare: Global content delivery network, edge security, and DDoS protection.
  • Google Workspace & Google Sheets: Operational lead coordination and class scheduling.
  • Notion: Internal CRM and career counseling session coordination.

5. Data Retention & Security

We maintain appropriate technical, organizational, and encryption safeguards to prevent unauthorized data access, loss, or disclosure. We retain inquiry information only for as long as necessary to provide counseling services, support active course enrollments, or satisfy statutory legal requirements.

6. Your Privacy Rights & Grievance Officer

You have the right to request access to your personal data, request corrections to inaccurate information, withdraw your consent, or request complete erasure of your records from our database.
