Privacy & Transparency

Cookie Policy

Effective Date: October 2026. This policy explains how RPAVault uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work properly, provide a smoother browsing experience, and deliver aggregate reporting insights to website operators.

2. The Types of Cookies We Use

RPAVault uses the following categories of cookies:

  • Essential & Functional Cookies: Necessary for core site functionality, including maintaining form state, preventing cross-site request forgery, and storing user UI preferences (such as dismissed notices or chat widget toggles).
  • Analytics & Performance Cookies (Google Analytics 4): Help us understand how visitors interact with our pages, identify popular IT courses, and detect broken links or performance bottlenecks. All analytics data is aggregated and anonymized.
  • Marketing & Attribution Cookies (Meta Pixel): Used to measure the effectiveness of our training advertisements on Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram) and ensure prospective learners see relevant course tracks based on their interests.

3. Third-Party Services We Rely On

Our website utilizes trusted third-party providers that may place cookies on your device:

  • Google LLC: Google Analytics (traffic analysis) and Google Fonts (typography).
  • Meta Platforms, Inc.: Meta Pixel (ad conversion tracking).
  • Cloudflare, Inc.: CDN security and bot protection.

4. Managing and Disabling Cookies

You have full control over cookie usage. You can adjust your browser settings to reject non-essential cookies, clear existing cookies, or notify you when a cookie is placed. Note that disabling essential cookies may impact certain site functionality, such as form auto-fills or chat preferences.

For more information on managing cookies in popular browsers, consult your browser's help documentation (Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, or Microsoft Edge).

5. Contact & Questions

For questions about our use of cookies or privacy practices, please contact our team at info@rpavault.com or view our Privacy Policy.
