FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why Playwright over Selenium?

Playwright is faster, has built-in auto-waiting, better debugging (trace viewer) and first-class TypeScript support — and job postings are shifting to it rapidly. We cover the comparison in week one.

Do I need to know TypeScript?

No — the course teaches the TypeScript you need, specifically for test automation.

Will we cover CI/CD?

Yes — you'll run your test suites in GitHub Actions and Jenkins pipelines, exactly like SDET teams do.
