## Why certify at all?

A UiPath certification will not get you hired by itself — but it gets your resume past filters, and it forces you to close the gaps in your knowledge. Combined with a real project portfolio, it is a signal of capability.

## The certification ladder

* **Automation Developer Associate** — the standard entry certification; covers Studio, selectors, control flow, and REFramework basics.
* **Automation Developer Professional** — for working developers; advanced orchestration, error handling, and solution design.
* **Specialty paths** — Test Suite, Document Understanding, and agentic automation are growing fast.

## How to prepare

* Build 2–3 real projects — exam scenarios mirror real automation problems.
* Master selectors and debugging; most failed attempts trace back to these.
* Do timed mock exams in the final two weeks.
