## Why Your Power BI Portfolio Matters

If you want to land a data analyst job, certificates and resumes are no longer enough. Hiring managers want to see **what you can build**. They want to look at your dashboards, interact with your filters, and understand how you solve real business problems with data.

A good portfolio should show that you can clean dirty data, write optimized DAX measures, design intuitive layouts, and extract actionable business insights. Here are three high-impact Power BI projects you should build for your portfolio.

---

### Project 1: Executive Sales & Profitability Dashboard

This project demonstrates your ability to build a comprehensive dashboard for C-suite executives, tracking revenue, margin, and regional performance.

* **The Scenario:** A global retail company needs to monitor its sales performance across multiple channels, regions, and product categories.
* **Key Skills Showcased:**
  * **Data Modeling:** Creating a star schema with fact tables (Sales) and dimension tables (Customers, Products, Geography, Calendar).
  * **DAX Formulas:** Writing measures for Year-over-Year (YoY) Sales Growth, Running Totals, Profit Margin %, and Top N Products.
  * **UI/UX Design:** Implementing clean layouts with KPI cards, summary charts, and navigation buttons.
* **Real Data Source:** You can use the classic *AdventureWorks* or *Northwind* datasets, or download clean transactional retail datasets from Kaggle.

---

### Project 2: Customer Churn & Cohort Analysis

This project shows that you can help subscription or SaaS businesses identify which customers are leaving (churning) and why.

* **The Scenario:** A subscription-based service wants to analyze customer retention trends and target groups that have high churn rates.
* **Key Skills Showcased:**
  * **Advanced Data Cleaning (Power Query):** Cleaning subscription dates, handling missing values, and formatting customer cohorts.
  * **Time Intelligence DAX:** Creating cohort groups and calculating churn rate dynamically based on inactive months.
  * **Visual storytelling:** Using a cohort matrix heatmap and slicers to isolate customer segments (e.g., tenure, billing plan).

---

### Project 3: Financial Performance & Balance Sheet Report

This project proves you can tackle complex financial data layouts, which is a major asset for finance and consulting roles.

* **The Scenario:** A company needs to convert raw double-entry accounting ledger tables into a clean, interactive Balance Sheet and P&L statement.
* **Key Skills Showcased:**
  * **Custom Financial Layouts:** Formatting rows dynamically to show operating expenses, gross margins, and net income lines.
  * **Dynamic Slicers:** Letting users toggle between currency rates, quarters, or subsidiaries.
  * **Detail Tables:** Building drill-down transaction tables so analysts can inspect the individual ledger items behind any balance line.

---

### Tips for Publishing Your Portfolio

1. **Host on NovyPro or GitHub:** Since publishing to the web on Power BI Service requires a work account, you can create a free NovyPro profile or host your screenshots, data models, and DAX codes on a GitHub repository.
2. **Write a Case Study:** Don't just upload the `.pbix` file. Write a short explanation of the business problem, your dataset, your data modeling approach, and the key insights you discovered.
3. **Keep Design Clean:** Avoid using too many bright colors or confusing chart types (like 3D pie charts). Stick to a professional color palette and use plenty of white space.
