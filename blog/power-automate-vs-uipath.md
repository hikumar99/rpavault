## Two tools, two philosophies

**UiPath** is the enterprise RPA leader — deep, powerful, built for complex end-to-end automations with dedicated RPA developer roles. **Power Automate** is Microsoft’s automation layer — lighter, everywhere Microsoft 365 is, and often the first tool a company adopts.

## Where the jobs are

* UiPath: dedicated RPA Developer roles, automation CoEs, consulting projects.
* Power Automate: citizen-developer roles, Microsoft-shop IT teams, business analysts who automate.
* Both together: the profile staffing teams struggle hardest to find.

## Learning curve and salary

Power Automate is faster to start; UiPath goes deeper and pays more at the senior end — especially with agentic automation and AI agents in the mix.

## Our take
