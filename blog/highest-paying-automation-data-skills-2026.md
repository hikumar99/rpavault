## What the market is paying for

Hiring data across India in 2026 shows a consistent pattern: companies are done paying for tool knowledge alone. The premium goes to people who combine a **core tool**, a **data skill**, and increasingly an **AI layer**.

## The skills stack, ranked

* **Agentic RPA (UiPath + AI)** — the biggest jump; senior RPA developers who can build AI agents command 30–50% premiums.
* **SQL** — still the most-requested skill in data job descriptions, and the best salary-per-hour-of-learning ratio.
* **Power BI with advanced DAX** — dashboard builders are common; modelers who know DAX are not.
* **Playwright with TypeScript** — rapidly replacing Selenium in modern teams; early skills, less competition.
* **Power Automate** — the enterprise default inside Microsoft shops, often paired with UiPath.

## How to stack them

Pick one core track and go deep enough to build real projects — then add an adjacent skill. UiPath + SQL, or Power BI + SQL, beats five certificates with no portfolio.
