## The myth holding people back

Most people think an IT career starts with learning to code for a year. It does not. The fastest transitions we have seen among our 5,000+ students came through **low-code automation and data tools** — skills you can learn in weeks and demonstrate in a portfolio.

## The three easiest doors into IT

* **RPA / automation** — UiPath and Power Automate are visual, drag-and-drop tools; your domain knowledge from a non-IT job becomes an asset.
* **Data analytics** — SQL + Power BI turn business sense into dashboards companies pay for.
* **Software testing** — structured, learnable, and modern tools like Playwright are in demand.

## The 90-day plan

* Weeks 1–6: live training with real projects — not just videos.
* Weeks 7–10: build a portfolio of 2–3 projects you can demo.
* Weeks 11–13: resume, mock interviews, and applications — with placement support.

Your experience is not a liability — a banker who can automate reconciliation is worth more than a fresher who can only code. Start with our [Data Analytics](/course/data-analytics-power-bi-sql/) or [RPA Agentic](/course/rpa-agentic-uipath-power-automate/) track.
