Your Structured 4-Step Pathway

A step-by-step journey custom-tailored to guide you safely from a non-IT background to a successful technology placement.

1

Logic & System Fundamentals

We start with the absolute basics of business logic, variables, loops, data structures, and how software systems interact. No code is written here; we build your core analytical mindset first.

2

Low-Code Tools & Automation Mastery

Learn to design workflows and bots using UiPath and Microsoft Power Automate, and build business intelligence dashboards using Power BI and SQL Server. These are highly visual, drag-and-drop tools that command premium salaries.

3

The Hands-on Sandbox Labs

Work on 15+ mini-projects and 2 enterprise-scale Capstones (like automatic PDF invoice extraction or automated CRM onboarding) mapping real-world business bottlenecks.

4

Resume Rebranding & Direct Placements

Translate your previous industry domain experience (banking, insurance, retail) into valuable functional consulting assets on your resume. Practice mock interviews with IT leads and access our partner network placement portal.
