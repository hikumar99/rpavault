Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you enroll.

How long do the courses run?

Focused batches typically run 6–10 weeks depending on the track. Corporate workshops and college programs are scheduled around your team or campus calendar.

Are classes online or in-person?

Both. We run in-person sessions in Hyderabad and live online batches, so you can join from anywhere in India.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes — every program includes a completion certificate, and we guide you toward platform certifications like UiPath where relevant.

What are the course fees?

Fees vary by track and format. Contact us or request a callback and we will share the current batch pricing and dates.

Do you help with interviews and placements?

Every batch builds a real project portfolio, and we coach you on interviews. Through our staffing practice, strong performers get visibility into corporate openings.

Not sure which program fits?

Tell us your team, campus, or career goal — we will recommend the right track and format.
